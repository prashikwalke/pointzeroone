01

the idea

most people stop at 1%.

which is already good. good is where most journeys quietly end — not because people run out of ability, but because 1% is comfortable enough to stay in.

0.01 is not a number to reach. it's the distance between doing something well and doing something only you could have done. it is slower. it is less certain. almost nobody will tell you to go there.

i am not there. i'm somewhere on the way, mostly working it out as i go. this site is the record of that walk — kept honestly, in public, while it is still unfinished.

this journey from 1% to 0.01% is a journey of curious minds — people not afraid to take risks, and willing to actually work on the thing they really care about.
